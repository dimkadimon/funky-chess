# Funky Chess 🎮

Chess with creative twists! Play against an AI opponent with three unique game variants.

## 🎯 Variants

- **🌀 Teleports** - Two symmetric teleport squares appear on the board. Step on one to warp to the other!
- **🔄 Swapsies** - When you capture a piece, your piece transforms into that type. A pawn capturing a queen becomes a queen!
- **🧱 Walls** - Four wall squares block all movement through them. Plan your routes carefully!

## 🤖 AI Difficulty

- **Easy** - Quick casual play, depth 2 search
- **Medium** - Balanced challenge, depth 3 search  
- **Hard** - Serious opponent, depth 4 with quiescence search

## ✨ Features

- Full chess rules including castling, en passant, and pawn promotion
- Three creative game variants
- Alpha-beta pruning AI with piece-square tables
- Particle effects and screen shake
- Undo moves (Ctrl+Z)
- Move history panel
- Touch-friendly for mobile
- Keyboard shortcuts (Esc to pause)
- Responsive design
