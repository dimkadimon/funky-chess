# Funky Chess 🎮

Chess with creative twists! Play against an AI opponent with four unique game variants.

## 🎯 Variants

- **🌀 Teleports** - Two symmetric teleport squares appear on the board. Step on one to warp to the other! Capture enemies at the destination.
- **🔄 Swapsies** - When you capture a piece, your piece transforms into that type. King keeps its identity. Promotion overrides swap.
- **🧱 Walls** - Four wall squares block all movement through them. Plan your routes carefully!
- **🍩 Torus** - Board wraps left↔right. Pieces exit one edge and enter the other! Arrows indicate wrap points.

## 🤖 AI Difficulty

- **Easy** - Quick casual play, depth 2 search
- **Medium** - Balanced challenge, depth 4 search
- **Hard** - Serious opponent, depth 5 search

## ✨ Features

- Full chess rules including castling, en passant, and pawn promotion
- Four creative game variants
- Threefold repetition and 50-move rule detection
- Alpha-beta pruning AI with piece-square tables and mop-up evaluation
- Undo moves (Ctrl+Z)
- Move history panel with copy-to-clipboard
- Dark and light themes
- Touch-friendly for mobile
- Responsive design
