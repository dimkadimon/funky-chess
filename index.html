Funky Chess

Chess with a twist!

🌀 Teleports: Two symmetric teleport squares. Step on one to warp to the other!
🔄 Swapsies: Capture a piece? Your piece becomes that type!
🧱 Walls: Four wall squares block all movement.
🍩 Torus: The board wraps left↔right. Pieces can go off one edge and appear on the other!
